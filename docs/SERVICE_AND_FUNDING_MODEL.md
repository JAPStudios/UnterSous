# UnterSous Service and Funding Model

Status: planning baseline; not a legal or funding award determination.

## Service model

UnterSous intends to coordinate passenger transportation and delivery while paying participating drivers directly. Qualifying medical and legal transportation is intended to be free to passengers. Driver compensation remains an operating cost even when the passenger fare is zero.

## Separate service ledgers

The following must not be combined into one undifferentiated revenue or trip category:

1. General passenger transportation.
2. Medical transportation.
3. Legal transportation.
4. Delivery.
5. Driver training, licensing or employment-support activity.

Each category may have different eligibility, regulatory, insurance, contract, reimbursement and reporting requirements.

## Funding alignment

- Accessible transportation and mobility management: assess FTA Section 5310 eligibility, eligible applicant structure, match and regional priorities.
- Rural public transportation: assess FTA Section 5311 through an eligible municipal sponsor. A private operator may need to participate as a contracted provider rather than as the direct grant recipient.
- Medicaid-covered non-emergency medical transportation: assess provider enrollment and contracting with the applicable New York Medicaid transportation manager. Only authorized, covered trips qualify; this is not general-purpose app funding.
- Youth and workforce supports: assess WIOA youth and New York workforce programmes for qualifying participant training, work experience and support costs. Confirm local participant eligibility and allowable driver-education costs before budgeting.
- Training programme operations: assess New York State workforce grants only where the programme meets the grant's track-record, partnership, outcome and match requirements.
- Free legal transportation: identify a separate public contract, eligible legal-services partnership, philanthropy or unrestricted operating source. Medicaid medical transportation funding must not be assigned to legal journeys.

## Financial controls

- Identify the payer or funding source for each trip before classifying it as free to the passenger.
- Track restricted funds by award, contract, purpose, period and allowable cost.
- Allocate driver compensation, insurance, vehicle, dispatch and administrative costs using a documented method.
- Do not assume grant approval, reimbursement, contract award or participant eligibility.
- Do not promise a free ride until an operating budget or approved funding source can cover the trip and driver compensation.

## Operating readiness

Before live service, obtain jurisdiction-specific determinations for transportation authority, driver qualifications, vehicle standards, commercial insurance, employment classification, passenger safety, accessibility, privacy and any Medicaid or public-contract requirements.
